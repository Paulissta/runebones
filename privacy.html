Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what data the RuneBones game, this website and the RuneBones studio tools (together, "the Service") handle, and why.

The game

  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device (browser storage, or the desktop app's files).
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay analytics through GameAnalytics, such as journeys started, heroes picked and match results, with a random device id. It does not collect your name, email or contacts. You are told about it on your first visit and can turn it off at any time in the game's settings.

TikTok integration

This applies only if you choose to connect a TikTok account.

  • What we receive: your TikTok open id, avatar and display name (scope user.info.basic), and permission to send the videos you pick to your account (scopes video.upload and, if enabled, video.publish).
  • Why: to show you which account will receive the video, and to send the video, caption and options you chose. We use the data for nothing else.
  • Where it is kept: the access and refresh tokens stay only on the device running the tool. They are never stored on our servers, never sold and never shared.
  • How to remove it: revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. You can also delete the stored tokens from the tool, or ask us to help by email.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data goes only to the services needed for a feature to work (TikTok and GameAnalytics), under their own privacy policies.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no trackers; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
