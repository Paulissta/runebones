Every die you place is an attack.

A strategy dice duel inside a roguelike journey. Roll, drop your die into a column, match values to multiply your score, destroy your rival's dice, and spend energy on runes that twist the board.

Play free on itch.io

The game

Dice duels

You and your rival drop one die per turn. Matching values in a column multiply. The same value in the facing column destroys theirs.

19 runes

Aggressive, Builder, Control and Tempo runes flip enemy dice, lock columns, forge your dice or grant an extra turn.

20 rivals

Duelists, elites and six three-phase bosses, each a neon figure with its own rules and voice.

Your journey

Three acts on a branching map. Build your dice bag, collect bone charms, visit the market, the forge and roadside events.

Five heroes

The Wanderer, the Pyromancer, the Bone Collector, the Gambler and the Architect, each with its own bag and twist.

Play anywhere

Runs in your browser on desktop and phone. The free Demo has the whole three-act journey.
